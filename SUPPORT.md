# Rackup Support

Rackup helps you read a Crossplay screenshot or find words for one spot on the board.

## Import a screenshot

Copy an upright, light-theme Crossplay screenshot and tap Paste screenshot, or choose one from Photos or Files. Keep the full 15-by-15 board visible. Perspective photos, dark game themes, obscured boards, and almost-full boards with too few empty squares are not currently supported.

Tap any board or rack tile to correct it. Orange markers identify tiles that need review. Confirm those tiles before solving. If a finished-game screenshot has no visible rack, tap the empty rack slots to enter your letters.

## Find words by hand

Choose Find words, enter your rack, and add any existing letters to the line. Leave a space for each empty square, add bonus squares, and use a wall where there is no more room. Starts with and Ends with align the word with the first or last entered letter; Contains includes the entered letters. A line score does not include perpendicular words.

## Saves and purchases

Your entries save on the device. Deleting Rackup removes its local app data. The App Store manages purchases and refunds; Rackup does not have a separate account or subscription.

## Contact

[Open a support issue](https://github.com/tylergannon/rackup-support/issues/new) with your iPhone model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. Reports are public. Do not include private information or sensitive screenshots. For App Store billing or refund questions, use [Apple’s purchase support](https://support.apple.com/billing).
