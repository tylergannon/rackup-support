# Rackup Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026.

Rackup reads screenshots and finds words on your device. It does not send your screenshots, board positions, rack letters, or searches to a server.

Your board, rack, word-finder entries, and settings are saved in the app’s local storage. Rackup does not retain imported screenshots. Device backups may include the app’s saved data, depending on your system settings. Deleting the app removes its local data; you can manage backups separately in system settings.

Rackup has no advertising, analytics, accounts, or tracking SDKs. We do not collect personal data through the app.

You choose which image to import through the system Photos or Files picker. The clipboard is read when you tap Paste screenshot. Word definitions use the system dictionary. Apple provides these system services under its own privacy policies. Apple also processes your App Store purchase under its own terms and privacy policy; Rackup does not receive your payment details.

If you contact support, the information you choose to send will be used to respond to your request. Do not send screenshots containing private information unless you want that information included in your support request.

For help or privacy questions, [open a Rackup support issue](https://github.com/tylergannon/rackup-support/issues/new). This is a public forum, so do not include personal data. GitHub hosts these support pages and processes website requests under its [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
